> "When managing project bids, I issue standardized RFQ packets to wholesale fabricators and installers specifying all technical requirements (1:1 paper pattern, UL listing, crating, liftgate delivery). Once bids return:
> 1. I extract and normalize vendor pricing into a structured comparison spreadsheet in Excel or Google Sheets.
> 2. I audit line items for freight, crating, hardware, and turnaround lead times.
> 3. I apply the target gross margin formula (`Cost / (1 - GM%)`) rather than simple markup to ensure profitability is defended.
> 4. I assemble the approved line items into a clear, client-facing job proposal using established company templates."
