Technical Nuance

Platform Aspect Ratio & Specs Hook Truncation & Limits Safe Zones & Layout Hygiene Link & Distribution Strategy
LinkedIn 4:5 PDF Carousels (1080×1350); 1200×1200 image; 1:1 video Truncates at ~210 characters (3 lines). Hook must force the “…see more” click. Clean white/dark margins. High-contrast typography readable on mobile screens. External links placed in first comment or pinned profile section to protect algorithmic reach.
Instagram 9:16 Reels (1080×1920); 4:5 Feed Carousels (1080×1350) Truncates at 125 characters. Bold headline embedded on slide 1 / video cover. UI Safe Margins: Top 15% (header) and bottom 20% (captions/audio) kept clear. 3–5 curated niche hashtags; CTA directs audience to comment keyword for ManyChat DM routing.
YouTube 16:9 Long-Form (4K/1080p); 9:16 Shorts (<60s, 1080×1920) Titles <60 characters for mobile display. Timestamps and chapters in description. Shorts Safe Zone: Right edge (like/share buttons) and bottom-right (timestamp) kept clear. Pinned comment contains canonical UTM link; custom high-contrast thumbnail uploaded.
X (Twitter) 16:9 Media Cards (1200×675); Single 1:1 graphics 280 characters maximum. Concise punchy micro-hooks; 5–7 tweet threads. No border clutter. High-contrast single-point infographics. Thread links placed on tweet #1 or final CTA tweet. Scheduled auto-retweet at +12 hours for global reach.
